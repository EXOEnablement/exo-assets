# EXO ICP Scoring Rubric v1 — INTERNAL

Source: Nick's rubric, 2026-11-26. This is the internal prospect-scoring version.
The public calculator at exoenablement.github.io/icp-calculator.html is a buyer-facing
translation of the same weights — it is not this document.

## Scoring /100
- Function fit — 30
- Team reality — 25
- Authority — 15
- Timing signals — 20
- Pain evidence — 10

## Bands
- 70+ — Prospect now
- 50–69 — Watch
- <50 — Park

## Hard disqualifiers
- No outbound function
- Senior title with wrong function
- No UK/EMEA presence
- Direct competitor
- Contact has left the company

## Tiers
- Tier 1: SDR leaders, teams of 5+ SDRs (more profitable ideal — NOT a hard filter)
- Tier 2: Pre-hire / first-SDR readiness (accepted entry offer)

  team size, hiring and tenure need LinkedIn / company-page verification to score fully.
