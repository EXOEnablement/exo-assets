## The idea in Nick's words (from the post)
- Gamers have spent years crafting elite custom players: maximising
  abilities, fine-tuning strengths, debating trade-offs ("Speed over
  strength", "Agility over defense") — FIFA, NBA games, Fantasy Football,
  with visual breakdowns that assist impactful choices.
- Apply the same principles to coaching and developing SDR teams:
  visualise a team's strengths, gaps and growth like player stats.
- Explicitly NOT about competition — about custom-building your own
  version of elite performance.
- Closing line of the post: "What if enablement dashboards felt less like
  scorecards and more like player profiles?" / "Empowering Sales — It's
  In The Game"
- The post's images show radar/spider charts of skill attributes and
  side-by-side player-profile stat comparisons.

## Portal fit (Toni's mapping — brainstorm, not agreed)
- SDR login place: a personal player-profile view — radar chart across the
  self-assessment areas already in the portal (Discovery, Listening &
  trust, Call structure, Objection handling, Prospecting craft,
  Coachability), showing growth vs their OWN previous profile, not vs
  teammates. Trade-off framing fits the one-focus-skill model: you are
  choosing what to level up next.
- Manager place: team shape at a glance (strengths/gaps across the team
  as profiles), aggregate only, consistent with the privacy tiers.
- Guardrail from Nick's own framing: development, not competition.
  No leaderboards, no rep-vs-rep ranking — that would break the
  anti-surveillance / protect-the-experience principle.
