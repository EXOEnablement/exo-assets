# Nooks website — design inspiration (Nick's screenshots, 2026-11-27)

Status: INSPIRATION ONLY — patterns and layout ideas, not code, not copy, not branding.
Nooks is a proprietary reference studied for behaviour/layout only, like the other
reference platforms. Never referenced in anything public-facing.

Nick sent three screenshots during the site/portal design work. Breakdown:

## 1. Proof / outcomes bento grid
- Eyebrow: "OUTCOMES SPEAK LOUDEST" — small caps, accent word in colour.
- Huge tight sans headline: "Agents that deliver. Numbers that prove it." — one word in accent colour.
- Bento grid of rounded cards: customer-rating card, large photo/video hero card,
  client-logo tiles, a saturated accent block with a big stat pill
  ("30% -> 120% Low-performer attainment"), testimonial card with oversized stat
  ("+31% MEETINGS"), attribution as pills (name + role).
- EXO mapping: a proof bento on the homepage using ONLY Nick's real proof —
  300+ workshops, +60% engagement, 50+ SDRs, 5 regions — numbers gold, labels white bold
  (per Nick's stat rule). Testimonial card = a recommendation quote (Mandela / Lihua / Shawn,
  already on site). Accent block in burgundy, accent word/stat in gold. No client logos —
  Nick cannot claim logos he doesn't have; regions/tools tiles instead if needed.

## 2. Numbered stage pills + switching panel
- Horizontal pill tabs: "01 Inbound" (active = filled accent), 02 Outbound,
  03 Deal Management, 04 Expansion, 05 Coaching & Performance.
- Panel below swaps content per stage: left = headline, plain-language description,
  small-caps feature list, one big stat with a small tag; right = product visual.
- EXO mapping: this is the strongest fit. EXO's four onboarding stages as pills —
  01 Day One, 02 Week One, 03 Month Three, 04 Promotion — switching a panel with
  what happens, what the manager does, and one proof point per stage.
  Works on the site ("Your team" / What I do) AND mirrors the portal's stage structure.
  Also a direct answer to Nick's "the website feels stagnant" — this moves.

## 3. Dark research panel
- One huge rounded-corner near-black panel containing everything.
- Big white headline ("Nooks Research"), subline with a single accent word,
  small right-aligned description + "Discover" link with a circular arrow button.
- Fading dot-grid pattern, scattered rounded-corner photos of real people
  at varied sizes/positions — human, editorial, not stocky.
- EXO mapping: an EXO philosophy / Manifesto panel — dark burgundy panel, gold dot grid,
  headline in white with one gold word, photos of Nick coaching/workshops when available,
  link through to EXO writing/benchmarking. Fits "Hard on the problem. Invested in the person."

## Cross-cutting observations
- Typography: large, tight, modern grotesque SANS headlines throughout — no serif.
  Directly relevant to Nick's repeated "the font and size is wrong" on the EXO site:
  our Georgia serif headlines read older/weaker next to this. Recommendation to test:
  sans for headlines, keep body as is, compare on the homepage hero first.
- Rounded corners everywhere (cards ~24px, panel ~40px) — softens an otherwise
  aggressive sales-tech look; would take the hard edge off our flat rectangles.
- One accent colour used with discipline: accent = one word, one pill, one stat block.
  Matches Nick's gold-restraint rule (gold for numbers/earned elements, white for text).
- Floating pill CTA bottom-right ("Ask me how Nooks drives results") — EXO equivalent
  could be a persistent "Score your team" / diagnostic pill. Brainstorm only, not decided.

## Not to take
- Pink/saturated candy palette, AI-agent messaging, vendor logos, and any leaderboard/
  competition framing — all off-brand or off-philosophy for EXO.
