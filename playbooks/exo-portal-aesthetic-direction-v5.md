# EXO portal + website — aesthetic direction v5 (Nick, voice call 2026-11-27)

## The verdict on the current look
Nick: the current site/portal look is "very juvenile" — everything, not one
element. A full aesthetic rethink, not tweaks.

## Why gamification stays (Nick's reasoning, confirmed in his words)
- New hires coming into sales have grown up fluent in a visual gaming
  mentality: levels, rankings, attributes, skills — all visible.
- Businesses don't offer that. EXO should tap into that mentality.
- This generation is highly capable of taking on large amounts of
  information — it just needs structuring in a way that works for them.
- Core principle: MEET PEOPLE WHERE THEY ARE. Break away from old
  stereotypical approaches.
- Progress must be VISIBLE to the person: they can see how real
  information and real training impacts their growth.
- Nick confirmed Toni's summary: visible, personal growth in a grown-up,
  serious presentation — not a kids' game, not a public leaderboard.

## Aesthetic references (behaviour/look study only — no assets, copy or
branding taken from any of them)
1. Hard Skill Exchange (hardskill.exchange) — the register: dark, premium,
   editorial, skills presented like serious market data. Executive tone.
2. Attensi (attensi.com) — the proof game mechanics can look corporate:
   realistic simulation visuals, polished product presentation, enterprise
   proof; points/levels are the mechanism underneath, not a cartoon surface.
3. Ascent Cloud / LevelEleven — the gamification presentation layer:
   personal scorecards, manager drill-down, coaching tied to the numbers.
   Borrowed: scorecard clarity and presentation. NOT borrowed as the point:
   public leaderboards/contests — EXO's framing is development against
   your own previous profile (per Nick's player-profile post).

## Design rules that follow
- Dark burgundy/black ground, gold accents, editorial serif headlines —
  EXO colours, presented at HSE/Attensi polish level.
- Game language on the inside (levels, attributes, skill progression,
  player profile), grown-up presentation on the outside.
- Every visual element must answer: does this show the person their own
  growth, visibly, from real training/coaching data?
- Benchmark test: a screen passes if it could sit next to HSE or Attensi
  without embarrassing EXO.
